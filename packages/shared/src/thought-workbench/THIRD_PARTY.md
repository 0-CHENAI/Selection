`context.ts` adapts the layered structural/reference traversal in `src/lib/graph.ts`
and `src/store/context-builder.ts`. Selection replaces the graph representation,
indexes traversal, validates cycles, and hashes complete compiled input. Upstream
branding, remote services, desktop runtime and update mechanism are not included.
